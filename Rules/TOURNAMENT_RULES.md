# Overview
This document is designed to outline the tournament rules for the game of Redemption

# Basics
Types:
  * Two Player (aka 1v1)
  * Multiplayer (aka anything more than two players)

Rounds:
  * Each tournament consists of at least 2 rounds to be considered a tournament.

ELO
  * Much like chess this is a players standings for the year with respect to the rest of the players that year

Points:
  * The basis for ELO is that Redemption games are played to 5 points. The differential between players in a game also plays a fundamental role in calculating a player's ELO.

## Two Player Calculations
Redemption is played to 5 points when scoring a match the players total up their points and then the differental is calculated between those two players.
For example, player 1 is the winner at 5 points and player 2 is the loser with 3 points. This means the differential is 2 points in favor of player 1.
